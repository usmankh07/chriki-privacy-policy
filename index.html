1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application Learn Fusha Arabic - Chriki ("the Application"), developed by us. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency about how your data is handled when you use our educational app.

2. Information Collection and Use

We do not collect, transmit, sell, or rent any personally identifiable information (PII).

  • User Accounts: You do not need to create an account or provide any personal details (such as your name, email address, or phone number) to use the Application.
  • Local Progress & Preferences: Learning progress, completed lessons, dialect selections, and preferences are stored strictly locally on your device (using on-device storage such as AsyncStorage). This data is never sent to any external server.
  • Audio & Media: The Application plays educational audio clips. If speech or microphone features are utilized for pronunciation practice, the audio is processed locally on your device in real time and is neither recorded, saved, nor transmitted to external servers.

3. Third-Party Services

The Application is built using the Expo / React Native framework and distributed via the Google Play Store. It may interact with standard system-level services provided by:

  • Google Play Services: Required for application distribution, basic device compatibility checks, and in-app updates according to Google's Privacy Policy.

We do not integrate any third-party analytics trackers, advertising networks, or data brokers that monitor your cross-app activity.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Because all user progress and preferences are stored locally on your device, you have complete control over your data:

  • You can clear your progress at any time by clearing the Application's cache/storage in your device settings.
  • Uninstalling the Application completely removes all associated local data from your device.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Google Families Policy)

Our Application is designed for language learners of all ages. We do not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe any personal information has inadvertently been collected, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly ensure its deletion.

6. Security

We value your trust. Since we do not transmit or store personal information on remote servers, your exposure to data breaches is minimized. We employ standard industry practices to ensure the integrity of the application package.
